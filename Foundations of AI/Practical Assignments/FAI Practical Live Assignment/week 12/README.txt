Project Name - Prisoner's Dilemma Strategy Simulator

Description - 

Always Cooperate: This strategy is purely "Cooperate" regardless of the opponent's behavior.
Always Betray: A purely selfish strategy that always chooses "Betray" to maximize potential short-term gains.  
Random: This behavior introduces unpredictability by choosing between cooperation and betrayal with a 50/50 probability.  
Tit-for-Tat: It starts with cooperation and thereafter mimics the opponent's previous move. Rewards cooperation and punishes betrayal.  
Human: Allows for manual decision-making to test your own intuition against the AI logic.

Reflection -

Cooperation: It is beneficial when both players trust each other and get 3 points but risky if the opponent betrays, resulting in 0 points.
Mutual Betrayal: When both players betray, they receive the lowest combined payout 1 point each.
